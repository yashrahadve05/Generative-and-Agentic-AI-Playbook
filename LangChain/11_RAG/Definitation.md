## **RAG -** Retrieval Augmented Generation is a way to make a language model (like ChatGPT) smarter by giving it extra information at the time you ask your question. Instead of just relying on what it already knows, RAG allows the model to look up relevant information from a large collection of documents or data sources to help it answer your question better.

## Components of RAG
1. Document Loaders
2. Text Splitters
3. Vector Stores
4. Retrievers
5. LLMs
